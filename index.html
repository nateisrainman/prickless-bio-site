Questions, answered

The honest FAQ.

Do I really not need a needle?
Correct. The Metabolic is two capsules a day, full stop. No injections, no prescriptions, no clinic visits. The whole point of Prickless Bio is delivering the intent of peptide protocols in a format you'll actually keep up with.
How does liposomal delivery actually work?
Each active is wrapped in a phospholipid bilayer, a microscopic bubble made of the same kind of lipid as your cell membranes. That shell protects the botanical from stomach acid and gut proteases and helps carry it into circulation, instead of being broken down before it can be absorbed.
What should I expect, and when?
The Metabolic is designed for daily, consistent use to support healthy metabolism over time. It isn't a stimulant or a quick fix. Everyone's biology is different, so we suggest giving it a full cycle. Consistency with your AM and PM capsules matters more than any single dose.
Is it safe to take every day?
Every active is dosed within established clinical ranges and each batch is third-party tested for purity and potency. As with any supplement, check with your healthcare provider before starting, especially if you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, or managing a health condition.
Is it vegan and non-GMO?
Yes. The capsule is plant-based (HPMC), the phospholipid base is non-GMO sunflower lecithin, and there's no gluten, dairy, or soy. Nothing synthetic, nothing animal-derived.
How do I use my HSA or FSA?
At checkout you'll complete a quick telehealth survey powered by TrueMed / Sika Health. If you qualify, you can pay with your HSA/FSA card and use pre-tax dollars, with no separate paperwork or reimbursement chase.
What's your guarantee?
